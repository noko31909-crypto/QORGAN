# Qorgan Summary

Schools need faster help when danger appears. Today, people often lose time because warnings are late, information is unclear, and response steps are not organized.

Qorgan is a school safety solution designed to fix that. It watches school camera feeds and quickly warns security staff when there may be a weapon. Instead of relying on one person to notice a problem, Qorgan sends the same alert to the right people right away.

From there, the team can act in a clear order. Each incident moves through three stages: new, acknowledged, and resolved. This helps everyone know what is happening now, who has seen it, and when the situation is closed.

Qorgan also keeps all important updates in one place, so school teams can review what happened and learn from each event. Staff can add notes, track actions, and monitor response quality over time.

What makes Qorgan different is that it is not only a warning tool. It is a complete response flow: detection, alert, action, and follow-up in one system. It also includes emergency support features like SOS and safety guidance, so schools are better prepared before and during critical moments.

In short, Qorgan helps schools respond faster, stay coordinated, and protect students and staff with a practical system built for real-world use.
