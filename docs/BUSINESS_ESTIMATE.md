# Qorgan Financial Model (Conservative Version)

This version is designed for judge scrutiny. It includes assumptions, risks, and realistic school procurement constraints.

## 1) Core Pricing Assumptions
- Model: B2B annual subscription by school size and camera count.
- Pilot: discounted pilot with clear success criteria and conversion rules.
- No ad-based monetization and no student data monetization.

### Suggested Annual Pricing
| School profile | Cameras | Annual price (USD) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Small | 1-3 | 1,200-2,000 |
| Medium | 4-8 | 2,500-4,000 |
| Large | 9+ | 5,000-8,000 |

## 2) Cost Structure Assumptions

### Per-school first-year costs
| Cost item | Range (USD) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Onboarding and setup | 300-900 | integration + user training |
| Infrastructure allocation | 200-700 | depends on camera load and uptime |
| Support and maintenance | 300-900 | incident handling + updates |
| Compliance and governance overhead | 100-400 | policy, documentation, controls |
| **Total first-year cost** | **900-2,900** | conservative range |

### Ongoing annual costs from year 2
| Cost item | Range (USD) |
|---|---:|
| Infra + support + governance | 500-1,800 |

## 3) Unit Economics Example (Medium School)
| Metric | Conservative | Expected | Optimistic |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Annual revenue | 2,500 | 3,000 | 4,000 |
| First-year total cost | 2,400 | 1,700 | 1,100 |
| First-year gross margin | 4% | 43% | 72% |
| Year-2+ gross margin | 28% | 63% | 82% |

Key point: first-year margins can be thin if onboarding/support are underestimated.

## 4) Sales and Conversion Assumptions
- Average school sales cycle: 3-9 months.
- Pilot-to-paid conversion target: 30-50%.
- Renewal target after first paid year: 70-85%.

If conversion is below 25%, the business is not sustainable without grant support.

## 5) Growth Scenarios

### Scenario A: 10 schools
- Revenue range: 20,000-35,000 USD
- High risk: support and onboarding can consume most margin.

### Scenario B: 50 schools
- Revenue range: 100,000-180,000 USD
- Better economics from shared support and infrastructure.
- Requires stronger operations, partner onboarding process, and service reliability.

## 6) What Judges Care About
- Are assumptions transparent?
- Are risks acknowledged?
- Is there a path from pilot to recurring paid contracts?
- Is the model ethical (no exploitative monetization)?

## 7) Pitch-Ready Financial Statement
Use this sentence in your pitch:
"Qorgan uses a school subscription model with conservative first-year assumptions. We prioritize pilot conversion and long-term retention over aggressive margin claims, because trust and reliability drive adoption in school safety."
